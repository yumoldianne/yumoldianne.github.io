hi, i'm dianne!

occasional wanderer

based in the philippines

I'm a consultant and data scientist.

my professional philosophy is driven by curiosity. i've always been somebody who loves discovering new things (be it hobbies or places), tinkering with personal projects, and having something to do with their hands. most of the time, i give myself new problems just because i accidentally wandered into something and couldn't stop thinking about it.

i do love solving problems, whether that be through technical or creative means, and that's what drew me into consulting and data science. two very different fields that are essentially tackling the same question at their core: how can we solve this problem?

and as someone who gets bored easily, there has never been a dull day in this line of work. there's always something new to learn and once you get the hang of it, something new comes at you.

on tiktok, i heard of the term, "professional novice". someone coined this term as a catch-all phrase for people who love trying new things.

i hope i never outgrow the excitement of being a beginner. i hope i'm always cool enough to keep learning something new.

i completed my master's degree in data science at ateneo de manila university. i also completed my bs in applied math (data science) in the same university. when i have the time, i help out with different data science projects for different organizations.

outside of work, i am an active city dweller. i climb casually. i love musicals and books. my fuel of choice? you can't go wrong with coffee or hojicha. i don't think i'm built for matcha. aside from coffee and hojicha, tiramisu is one of my guilty pleasures and i hope to make it my branding one day.

i love exploring cities and finding little signs of affection from the people who inhabit them. you can find these in the coffee shops, restaurants, movie theaters, and even along the sidewalks. there's something about seeing how people make a place their own that i find endlessly fascinating.

life is so big and there is so much to see and adore.
